## 👋 Welcome

My name is Lynn Fisher. I am a designer, artist, and frontend CSS developer. I make weird, creative, and experimental projects for the web. 

Each year, I completely redesign my personal website (**lynnandtonic.com**) with something extremely clever, novel, and interactive, serving as an annual showcase of web experimentation, modern CSS, and nostalgic web design with a modern polish.

---

## 🚀 Featured Projects & Experiments

Here are some of the notable projects and creative showcases featured across my portfolio and work:

### 1. A Single Div (`a.singlediv.com`)
An ongoing CSS art experiment where complex illustrations, icons, and pop-culture characters are hand-drawn using **just one single HTML `<div>` element** and CSS properties.

### 2. Nestflix (`nestflix.fun`)
A pseudo-streaming service that catalogues and features fake movies and TV shows found *within* real movies and TV shows.

### 3. WhyAZ (`whyaz.com`)
An illustrated love letter and CSS art exploration of Arizona, featuring landmarks and symbols drawn entirely with CSS.

### 4. The Yearly Redesign Archive
Every year, lynnandtonic.com undergoes a complete conceptual and visual reinvention. Past iterations have experimented with layout grids, interactive games, micro-interactions, and retro web aesthetics.
